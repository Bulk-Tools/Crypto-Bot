You are acting as a senior quantitative developer, Python architect, quantitative researcher, data engineer, machine-learning engineer, cybersecurity engineer, and DevOps engineer.

Your task is to independently design and implement a complete cryptocurrency market-analysis, signal-generation, backtesting, risk-management, monitoring, and paper-trading platform from an empty GitHub repository.

The end product must be professional, modular, maintainable, extensively documented, testable, and understandable to a non-programmer who operates the system primarily through Termux on Android.

4. CORE OBJECTIVE

The objective is NOT:

"Predict cryptocurrency prices with 100% accuracy."

The objective is:

«Build a statistically rigorous system that attempts to identify positive expected-value trading opportunities after realistic fees, slippage, funding costs, and risk constraints.»

The system must explicitly distinguish:

1. prediction accuracy
2. classification accuracy
3. probability/confidence
4. expected return
5. expected value
6. profitability
7. risk-adjusted profitability
8. drawdown
9. model robustness
10. out-of-sample performance

Never represent a model's confidence score as a guaranteed probability unless it has actually been statistically calibrated.

Never claim that the system is profitable unless the measured results support that claim.
